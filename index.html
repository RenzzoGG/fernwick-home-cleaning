FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

Not at all. Many clients leave a key or door code. All instructions are stored securely and only shared with your assigned cleaner.

Do you bring your own supplies?

Yes. We bring eco-friendly products and professional equipment. If you prefer specific products, just tell us in your request.

Are your products safe for kids and pets?

Our standard kit uses plant-based, non-toxic products that are safe for children and pets once surfaces are dry.

What if I'm not happy with the result?

Let us know within 24 hours and we'll come back to re-clean the areas you're not satisfied with, at no extra cost.

How do I reschedule or cancel?

Reply to your confirmation email or call us. Changes are free with at least 24 hours' notice.

How is the final price calculated?

It depends on the service, your home's size and any add-ons. Your quote is a fixed price, so there are no surprises on cleaning day.
